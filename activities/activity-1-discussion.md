# Activity 1 — draft it, then judge it

**Time:** 5 minutes drafting · **Groups of three or four** · **No file to produce**

## Read the case

`activities/cases/case-01-first-order.md` — a one-line ticket, ten undecided
things.

## Draft, do not write

On paper or the whiteboard, agree:

1. **Problem** — in the customer's words, no solution.
2. **Solution** — the outcome they get.
3. **Three user stories** — numbered, as a / I want / so that.
4. **One decision** for the ADR, with two alternatives you rejected.

Five minutes. Rough is the point; you are not being scored on prose.

## Then

We walk a finished PRD and ADR for the same case, and score it with the ten-item
checklist. Keep your draft open: you will be asked which decision the sample made
that you missed, and which of yours is better.

## One rule

Do not try to write the whole document in five minutes. Decide the *shape* and
the *one decision*; the rest is what the sample is for.
